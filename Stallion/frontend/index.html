01 / THE PROBLEM

Every size,
a new quote.

Change a sofa's width by a few centimetres and the wood, foam, fabric and springs all change with it. Working that out by hand for every enquiry is slow, and estimates can vary from person to person.

Stallion does the arithmetic the same way every time, from the factory's own material rates — so a quote reflects the sofa that will actually be built.

    Developed in the Department of ________, SVCE Bengaluru.

    Stallion is a project developed at Sri Venkateshwara College of Engineering, Bengaluru. It takes the work a furniture factory does for every enquiry — reading the design, sizing it, costing it and drawing it — and turns it into one repeatable workflow.

    It brings several engineering disciplines together: computer vision models trained on sofa photographs, a cost engine that scales a real bill of materials to any size, and generators for technical drawings, 3D models and production CAD files.

    The aim is practical — giving furniture makers a faster, more consistent way to answer the question every customer asks first: what will it cost?
